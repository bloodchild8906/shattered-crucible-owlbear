# The Shattered Crucible - Campaign Book and Artwork

This directory contains the most recent illustrated 94-page campaign PDF, 60 high-resolution PNG illustrations, the original reference contact-sheet assets, and source encounter maps.

Files:
- The_Shattered_Crucible_Illustrated_Complete.pdf - complete campaign setting and rules, including creature charges, loot/harvest, the undying true-dragon soul cosmology, and human rarity.
- artwork/maps/ - world and regional maps.
- artwork/creatures/ - twelve newly illustrated homebrew creatures.
- artwork/items/ - equipment and magic items.
- artwork/species/ and artwork/classes/ - portraits.
- encounter-maps/ - nine original manuscript encounter maps.
- reference-assets/ - original smaller cropped artwork and manifest.
- asset_manifest.json - SHA-256 inventory.

Illustrated region names sometimes differ from the core campaign settlements. Keep them distinct unless the GM deliberately adapts them.

The illustrated campaign PDF is the canonical guide; images are supplied separately for tabletop integrations.
